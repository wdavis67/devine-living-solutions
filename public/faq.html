General Questions

Select any question below to view the answer.

What type of housing does Devine Living Solutions provide?

Devine Living Solutions provides shared residential group housing for adults in Columbia, South Carolina and surrounding communities.

Our role is limited to housing and the household amenities specifically described on this website.

Do you offer short-term and long-term housing?

Housing arrangements may include short-term, long-term, or permanent housing, depending on availability, household compatibility, funding, and suitability for the available shared residential setting.

Who may be considered for housing?

We may consider seniors, veterans, adults with disabilities, individuals in recovery, returning citizens, low-income adults, adults with special needs, and other adults seeking shared residential housing.

Every referral is reviewed individually to determine whether the available housing may be an appropriate residential fit.

Does Devine Living Solutions provide medical care, personal care, or supervision?

No. Devine Living Solutions provides housing only.

We do not provide medical care, personal care, nursing services, supervision, medication management, behavioral-health services, therapy, case management, transportation, or other direct support services.

Do you accept residents who use outside service providers?

A prospective resident may independently use outside providers when appropriate and when the individual remains suitable for the available shared residential environment.

Any medical, personal-care, behavioral-health, therapeutic, case-management, transportation, medication-related, or other outside services must be arranged separately through independent third-party providers.

Those outside services are not provided by Devine Living Solutions.

What is included at move-in?

New residents receive a basic move-in welcome kit that may include:

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Bar of soap
  • Lotion and deodorant
  • Lip balm
  • Hand sanitizer and tissue
  • Nail clipper and nail file
  • Face cloth
  • Shampoo and conditioner

Providing these items does not constitute personal-care services.

What household supplies are provided?

Household essentials may include:

  • Laundry detergent
  • Paper towels
  • Toilet paper
  • Trash bags
  • Cleaning supplies
  • Dishwashing liquid
What housing amenities are included?
  • Washer and dryer access
  • Television in the community area
  • Internet and Wi-Fi
  • Furnished shared living spaces
  • Kitchen and dining areas
  • Utilities included
Who may submit a housing referral?

Housing referrals may come from:

  • Hospitals
  • Case managers
  • Social workers
  • Community organizations
  • Veterans organizations
  • Healthcare providers
  • Rehabilitation facilities
  • Family members
  • Individuals seeking housing
Can a referral come from outside South Carolina?

Yes. A referral may come from outside South Carolina when the prospective resident is seeking housing in the Columbia, South Carolina area.

Housing availability and placement are still based on the individual referral and the available shared residential setting.

What area does Devine Living Solutions serve?

Devine Living Solutions provides residential group housing in Columbia, South Carolina and surrounding communities.

How do I begin the referral process?

Visit the Referral page and provide the requested housing-related information about the prospective resident.

You may also contact us by phone or email with questions about housing availability or the referral process.

Is housing guaranteed after submitting a referral?

No. Submission of a referral does not guarantee acceptance or housing placement.

Referrals are reviewed individually for housing availability, compatibility, funding, and suitability for the available shared residential environment.

What if a resident needs services beyond housing?

Any services needed beyond housing and the household amenities described on this website must be arranged separately through independent third-party providers.

Devine Living Solutions does not provide or represent itself as the provider of those outside services.

Housing Only — Important Reminder

Devine Living Solutions provides residential housing only. We are not a medical, nursing, personal-care, home-health, behavioral-health, therapeutic, rehabilitation, or case-management provider.

We do not provide medical care, personal care, nursing services, supervision, medication management, behavioral-health services, therapy, case management, transportation, or other direct support services.

Residents who require services beyond housing and the household amenities described on this website must arrange those services separately through independent third-party providers.
