We provide safe, stable shared residential housing for adults in Columbia, South Carolina and surrounding communities.

Housing Only — No Direct Care or Support Services Provided

Serving Columbia, South Carolina and Surrounding Communities

Devine Living Solutions provides residential group housing in Columbia, South Carolina and surrounding communities.

Our focus is simple: providing adults with a clean, stable, respectful, and structured shared residential environment. Our role is limited to housing and the household amenities specifically described on this website.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, stable, and respectful housing that promotes dignity and greater housing stability.

Why We Exist

Devine Living Solutions was created to help address housing instability by providing adults with clean, respectful, and structured places to live.

We believe a dependable place to live can provide an important foundation for stability, independence, and connection to the community.

Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining comfortable shared homes, treating residents with dignity and respect, and clearly communicating the housing arrangements and household expectations.

Each referral is reviewed individually for housing availability, compatibility, funding, and suitability for a shared residential setting.

Our Core Values

These principles guide how we provide housing and interact with residents, families, and referral partners.

Dignity

Every resident deserves to be treated with respect, courtesy, and consideration.

Safety

We strive to maintain clean, organized, and respectful shared residential environments.

Stability

Our housing provides adults with a dependable place to live and establish greater residential stability.

Independence

Our housing model respects residents' independence within a shared residential environment.

Respect

We value respectful communication between residents, families, referral sources, and housing staff.

Community

Residents may independently access community resources and outside services that are available to them.

Housing Only — Important Information

Devine Living Solutions provides residential housing only. We are not a medical, nursing, personal-care, home-health, behavioral-health, therapeutic, or case-management provider.

We do not provide medical care, personal care, nursing services, supervision, medication management, behavioral-health services, therapy, case management, transportation, or other direct support services.

Residents who require services beyond housing and the household amenities described on this website must arrange those services separately through independent third-party providers.
