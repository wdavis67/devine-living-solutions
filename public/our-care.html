Our Housing

Our residential housing environment is centered on safety, stability, dignity, respect, independence, and shared-home compatibility.

A Respectful Shared Residential Environment

Devine Living Solutions provides shared residential housing for adults who can live safely and appropriately in a community-style home.

Dignity and Respect

Residents are treated with courtesy, consideration, privacy, and respect within the shared residential environment.

Housing Stability

A dependable place to live can provide a foundation for greater residential stability and consistency.

Independence

Our housing model respects each resident's independence within the expectations of a shared residential setting.

Shared-Home Safety

Referrals are reviewed to determine whether the available residential environment may be an appropriate and safe fit.

Community Living

Shared housing provides opportunities for residents to live respectfully alongside others while maintaining reasonable household expectations.

Clear Housing Expectations

We communicate housing arrangements, household expectations, availability, and other housing-related information as part of the placement process.

Individual Housing Review

Every prospective resident is considered individually. Referral information helps us determine whether the available housing may be appropriate for the individual's housing needs and the shared residential environment.

Factors We Consider

  • Current housing needs
  • Housing availability
  • Household compatibility
  • Suitability for shared residential living
  • Relevant mobility or accessibility considerations
  • Funding or payment arrangements
  • Outside services already arranged, when applicable
  • Accuracy of referral information

Outside Services Are Separate From Housing

Some residents may independently receive medical, personal-care, behavioral-health, therapeutic, case-management, transportation, or other services from qualified outside providers.

Those services must be arranged separately and are not provided by Devine Living Solutions.

Housing Only — Important Information

Devine Living Solutions provides residential housing only. We are not a medical, nursing, personal-care, home-health, behavioral-health, therapeutic, rehabilitation, or case-management provider.

We do not provide medical care, personal care, nursing services, supervision, medication management, behavioral-health services, therapy, case management, transportation, or other direct support services.

Residents who require services beyond housing and the household amenities described on this website must arrange those services separately through independent third-party providers.

A housing referral may not be appropriate when an individual's needs cannot be safely accommodated within the available shared residential environment.
