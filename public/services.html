Outside Community Services

Devine Living Solutions does not provide medical, personal-care, behavioral-health, therapeutic, case-management, transportation, or other direct support services.

Residents who require outside services must arrange them separately through independent third-party providers. Depending on eligibility and provider availability, outside services may include healthcare, therapy, behavioral-health services, case management, social work, medication assistance, durable medical equipment, transportation, and other community-based services.
