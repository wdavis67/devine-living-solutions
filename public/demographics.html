Who We Serve

We provide shared residential housing for adults seeking a safe, stable, structured, and respectful place to live.

Housing Only — No Direct Care or Support Services Provided

Residential Housing for Diverse Adult Populations

Devine Living Solutions provides residential group housing for adults in Columbia, South Carolina and surrounding communities. Each referral is reviewed individually for housing availability, compatibility, funding, and suitability for a shared residential setting.

Referrals may come from individuals, families, hospitals, case managers, social workers, community organizations, healthcare providers, veterans organizations, rehabilitation facilities, and other referral sources.

Populations We May House

Housing availability is considered individually based on the prospective resident's housing needs and suitability for our shared residential environment.

Seniors

Residential housing may be available for older adults seeking a safe, stable, and respectful shared living environment.

Veterans

Residential housing may be available for veterans seeking greater housing stability and a structured shared residential environment.

Adults With Physical Disabilities

Housing may be considered for adults with physical disabilities when the individual can live safely in the available residential environment.

Adults With Intellectual or Developmental Disabilities

Housing may be considered when the individual can live safely in a shared residential setting and any required outside services are arranged independently.

Individuals in Recovery

Residential housing may be available for individuals in recovery who are seeking a stable shared living environment. Any recovery, treatment, counseling, or related services must be provided separately by outside providers.

Returning Citizens

Housing may be available for adults returning to the community who are seeking a stable place to live.

Low-Income Adults

Residential housing options may be available for adults seeking a more stable shared living arrangement, subject to availability and an acceptable funding plan.

Adults With Special Needs

Housing may be considered when the prospective resident can live safely in a shared residential setting and independently arrange any outside services they require.

Adults Seeking Shared Residential Housing

Housing may be appropriate for adults who can safely participate in a shared residential environment and follow reasonable household expectations.

What Helps Make a Housing Placement Appropriate?

Each referral is reviewed individually to determine whether our available housing is an appropriate residential fit.

Shared-Living Suitability

The prospective resident should be able to live safely and respectfully in a shared residential environment.

Housing Compatibility

The available home and household environment should be reasonably compatible with the prospective resident.

Outside Services Arranged Separately

Any medical, personal-care, behavioral-health, therapeutic, case-management, transportation, or other support services must be arranged separately through independent third-party providers.

Stable Funding Plan

Housing arrangements should include a clear and dependable payment or funding plan.

Accurate Referral Information

Complete and accurate information helps us determine whether the available housing may be an appropriate fit.

Household Fit

Consideration is given to housing availability, household compatibility, and the nature of the shared residential environment.

Housing Only — Important Information

Devine Living Solutions provides residential housing only. We are not a medical, nursing, personal-care, home-health, behavioral-health, therapeutic, rehabilitation, or case-management provider.

We do not provide medical care, personal care, nursing services, supervision, medication management, behavioral-health services, therapy, case management, transportation, or other direct support services.

Prospective residents who require services beyond housing and the household amenities described on this website must arrange those services separately through independent third-party providers.

A housing referral may not be appropriate when an individual's needs cannot be safely accommodated within the available shared residential environment.

Who May Submit a Housing Referral?

Housing referrals may come from a variety of sources, including:

  • Hospitals
  • Case managers
  • Social workers
  • Community organizations
  • Veterans organizations
  • Healthcare providers
  • Rehabilitation facilities
  • Family members
  • Self-referrals

Submit a housing referral or contact us to discuss availability and whether the prospective resident may be appropriate for our shared residential environment.
